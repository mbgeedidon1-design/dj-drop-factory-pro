❓ Frequently Asked Questions
What is AI Drop mode? ▼
AI Drop mode uses intelligent script generation to create polished drops based on your DJ identity, genre, mood, and energy. It is designed for club sets, radio promos, and event branding.
How do I use My Script mode? ▼
Switch to "My Script" mode, type your own custom script in the text area, choose your genre and voice settings, then tap Generate Drop. You can also use the String Tools to auto-format your text.
Can I record my own voice? ▼
Yes! Tap the microphone icon in the Voice Recorder section to record your voice. After recording, you can apply effects like Helium, Robot, Echo, and more before using it as a background track.
Where are my saved drops stored? ▼
Your drops are saved in the Library tab. When online, they sync to the server. When offline, they are stored locally on your device and will sync once you are back online.
What audio formats are supported? ▼
The app generates broadcast-ready MP3 audio with professional-quality controls. Voice recordings can be refined with effects for branded mixes and polished presentations.
How do I use the Discover tab? ▼
The Discover tab lets you explore DJ groups, streaming apps, DJ software, festivals, and theater streaming platforms. Use the search bar to find specific DJs, apps, or events.
Is this app free to use? ▼
Yes. DJ Drop Factory Pro gives you a premium creative workspace with AI generation, voice controls, effects, and a library for organizing your best drops.
💡 Quick Tips
🎚️ Energy Level
Higher energy (8-10) works best for club bangers and hype drops. Lower energy (3-5) is great for smooth radio IDs.
🎤 Voice Selection
"Auto by Genre" automatically picks the best voice profile based on your selected genre for optimal results.
✨ FX Modes
"Auto" adapts effects to your genre. "Heavy" and "Insane" add aggressive processing — perfect for trap and dancehall drops.
